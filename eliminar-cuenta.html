1 ¿Cómo eliminar la cuenta desde la App Móvil?

  1. Abre la aplicación Ride Delivery en tu teléfono.
  2. Inicia sesión con tu cuenta.
  3. Toca el icono de Perfil (abajo a la derecha) > Ajustes.
  4. Presiona el botón rojo "Eliminar Cuenta Definitivamente" y confirma.
  5. Tu cuenta y datos se eliminan en ese mismo instante.

Account & Data Deletion

You can request and execute the permanent deletion of your Ride Delivery account directly through this live web portal, or via the mobile application settings.

Direct Account Deletion Portal

Enter your registered account credentials to authenticate and immediately trigger the purge of your data from our production databases.

Are you sure you want to permanently delete your account? This action is irreversible and will erase your profile, ride history, and system access.

1 How to delete account within the mobile app?

  1. Open the Ride Delivery app on your device.
  2. Sign in with your registered account.
  3. Go to Profile (bottom right) > Settings.
  4. Tap the red button labeled "Eliminar Cuenta Definitivamente" (Delete Account Permanently) and confirm.
  5. Your account is instantly purged from our servers.
